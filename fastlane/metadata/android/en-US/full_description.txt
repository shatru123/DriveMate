DriveMate is an intelligent, profile-aware car companion designed for drivers who value truthful vehicle telemetry, driver safety, and seamless voice assistance.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

🚗 Multilingual Voice Assistant
• Natural voice control in English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी)
• Understands conversational code-switching (Hinglish and Maranglish)
• Instant voice queries for vehicle speed, range, odometer, fuel, weather, expenses, parking, and maintenance
• Safe audio ducking and single-owner microphone lifecycle

📊 Truthful Vehicle Telemetry
• Direct integration with Car Hardware / OBD2 / GPS sensors
• Zero hallucinations: strict distinction between live vehicle sensors, GPS estimates, stale readings, and unavailable states
• Live speed, fuel level, range estimation, and trip statistics

👤 Multi-User Isolation & Single Vehicle Profile
• Secure on-device user account system
• Clean isolation of personal vehicle profiles, driving logs, documents, and settings
• Fast switching and offline data security

📁 Encrypted Automotive Document Vault
• Keep vehicle registration (RC), insurance policy, PUC certificate, and driver license handy
• Expiry date alerts and quick offline verification

🅿️ Smart Parking Assistant & Expense Tracking
• Automatic or one-tap parking location recall with GPS coordinates and notes
• Comprehensive fuel, maintenance, toll, and trip expense logging with categorized breakdown

🛡️ Privacy & Driver Safety First
• 100% on-device data processing — no proprietary tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Built strictly according to official Android for Cars safety guidelines
• Open-source weather powered by Open-Meteo with no API keys or telemetry

DriveMate is crafted for truthful, distraction-free driving companionship.
